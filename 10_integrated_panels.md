# Integrated multimodal panels

## 46. Paired magnification fungal invasion
**Image brief:** Panel A low-power H&E tissue showing a necrotic focus; Panel B higher-power special stain of the same focus with hyphae, folds, and pigment.

**Prompt:** Combining Panels A and B, is the filamentous structure in the necrotic focus tissue-invasive fungal hyphae or a surface processing artifact?

**Hardening target:** Requires registering overview location with high-power morphology.

## 47. Differential cell count from smear and scatter plot
**Image brief:** Panel A Wright-stained blood smear; Panel B flow-cytometry FSC/SSC scatter plot from the same specimen with gated populations and debris cloud.

**Prompt:** Which leukocyte population corresponds to the high-side-scatter cluster in Panel B and the cells with prominent cytoplasmic granules in Panel A?

**Hardening target:** Links morphology to cytometric granularity without relying on labels alone.

## 48. Before/after drug imaging
**Image brief:** Matched microscopy fields before and after treatment, same magnification and scale bar, with focus variation and a small stage shift.

**Prompt:** What is the dominant treatment-associated phenotype visible after treatment: reduced adherent-cell area, increased neurite length, or formation of a confluent monolayer?

**Hardening target:** Requires recognising the same cell population despite field shift.

## 49. Multipanel ELISA standard curve and plate image
**Image brief:** Panel A photographed ELISA plate with one bubble-containing well and edge effects; Panel B standard curve with unknown sample markers.

**Prompt:** Which unknown sample should be rejected before concentration interpolation because its photographed well contains an air bubble that compromises optical density?

**Hardening target:** Integrates physical plate artifact with plotted result.

## 50. Tumour–immune spatial relationship
**Image brief:** Multiplex immunofluorescence field with tumour marker, T-cell marker, nuclei, merge, and a corresponding low-power tissue map.

**Prompt:** In the high-power region identified on the tissue map, are the T-cell-marker-positive cells predominantly intratumoral, peritumoral stromal, or absent from the region?

**Hardening target:** Tests compartment boundaries and low/high-power panel alignment.
