# Image-derived pathology measurements

## 36. Tubular versus glomerular compartment
**Image brief:** PAS-stained renal cortex with glomeruli, proximal tubules, distal tubules, capillary loops, and a tissue fold.

**Prompt:** Which compartment in the image is identified by a round capillary tuft enclosed by Bowman’s space: glomerulus, proximal tubule, or collecting duct?

**Hardening target:** Tests spatial architecture instead of color alone.

## 37. Fibrosis proportion from trichrome
**Image brief:** Masson trichrome liver field with blue collagen, red cytoplasm, portal tract, and a blue staining crease; calibrated grid overlay is native to analysis export.

**Prompt:** Excluding the staining crease, which fibrosis pattern occupies the largest fraction of the analysed field: perisinusoidal, portal-based bridging, or absent?

**Hardening target:** Differentiates collagen architecture from fold artifact.

## 38. Ki-67 hot-spot selection
**Image brief:** Ki-67 IHC tumour section with several fields at identical magnification, brown nuclear positivity, pigment, necrosis, and edge staining.

**Prompt:** Which numbered viable-tumour field should be selected as the Ki-67 hot spot: the field with the greatest density of brown tumour nuclei, excluding necrosis and non-neoplastic lymphocytes?

**Hardening target:** Restricts counting to tumour nuclei.

## 39. Necrosis estimate
**Image brief:** H&E tumour cross-section with viable cellular rim, geographic necrosis, hemorrhage, empty processing clefts, and a scale bar.

**Prompt:** Which area should be included when estimating tumour necrosis: the acellular eosinophilic geographic region, the hemorrhagic region, or the empty processing clefts?

**Hardening target:** Separates necrosis from hemorrhage and artifactual spaces.

## 40. Vascular invasion criterion
**Image brief:** H&E tumour section showing a vessel with endothelial lining, intraluminal tumour embolus, retraction spaces, and red-cell-rich capillaries.

**Prompt:** Which visual finding establishes lymphovascular invasion: a tumour nest in an endothelial-lined space or a tumour nest separated from stroma by a non-lined retraction cleft?

**Hardening target:** Identifies an endothelial boundary without digital annotation.
