# Histopathology and immunohistochemistry

## 1. Invasive front versus retraction artifact
**Image brief:** High-power H&E field of breast carcinoma with irregular tumour nests, adipose tissue, and a focal clear space around nests; include true stromal infiltration elsewhere in the same field.

**Prompt:** In the provided H&E field, identify the single finding at the tumour–stroma interface that establishes **invasive carcinoma** rather than an in-situ lesion with retraction artifact. Give the histologic finding only.

**Hardening target:** Separates genuine infiltrative nests from empty clefts created by tissue processing.

## 2. Mitosis versus apoptotic body
**Image brief:** H&E high-power proliferative tumour field containing metaphase/anaphase figures, pyknotic apoptotic bodies, crushed nuclei, and staining precipitate.

**Prompt:** In the provided high-power H&E field, what is the morphologic category of the dark structure showing condensed chromosomes arranged in an equatorial plate and lacking a surrounding apoptotic halo?

**Hardening target:** Requires nuclear-architecture interpretation, not counting hyperchromatic dots.

## 3. Granuloma subtype
**Image brief:** H&E lung biopsy with epithelioid histiocytes, multinucleated giant cells, peripheral lymphocytes, and a central acellular granular zone; include adjacent fold artifact.

**Prompt:** Based on the central morphology of the granuloma in the image, classify it as **caseating** or **noncaseating**.

**Hardening target:** Distinguishes amorphous necrosis from pale tissue folds and washout.

## 4. IHC staining compartment
**Image brief:** DAB/hematoxylin IHC section with brown nuclear staining in tumour cells, brown granular cytoplasmic pigment in macrophages, and unstained stromal nuclei.

**Prompt:** In the tumour cells shown, which subcellular compartment contains the specific immunohistochemical signal: nuclear, cytoplasmic, or membranous?

**Hardening target:** Tests DAB localisation amid pigment and counterstain.

## 5. Papillary thyroid nuclear feature
**Image brief:** High-power thyroid H&E image with overlapping enlarged nuclei, grooves, inclusions, colloid, and focal air-drying artifact.

**Prompt:** Which nuclear feature visible in the lesional cells is most characteristic of papillary thyroid carcinoma: nuclear grooves, keratinization, or coarse chromatin clumping?

**Hardening target:** Requires associating a subtle internal nuclear contour with the correct cells.
