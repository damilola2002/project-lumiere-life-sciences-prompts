# Hematology

## 11. Schistocyte recognition
**Image brief:** Wright–Giemsa peripheral smear with helmet cells, keratocytes, bite cells, crenated cells, and platelet clumps.

**Prompt:** Which red-cell morphology visible in the image is a schistocyte: an irregular fragmented cell with two sharp horn-like projections, a uniformly crenated cell, or a semicircular bite cell?

**Hardening target:** Separates fragmentation from crenation and Heinz-body removal.

## 12. Malaria parasite stage
**Image brief:** Thin blood film with several erythrocytes, ring forms, platelets overlying red cells, stain precipitate, and one mature segmented form.

**Prompt:** What intraerythrocytic developmental stage is the parasite with multiple discrete merozoite-like chromatin bodies arranged around central pigment?

**Hardening target:** Distinguishes a schizont from superimposed platelets and multiple ring forms.

## 13. Acute leukemia lineage clue
**Image brief:** Bone-marrow aspirate smear with blasts, promyelocytes, Auer-rod-like needles, crushed cells, and azurophilic granules.

**Prompt:** The needle-shaped cytoplasmic inclusion visible in a blast supports which lineage: myeloid or lymphoid?

**Hardening target:** Requires identifying a true Auer rod rather than stain streaks.

## 14. RBC inclusion
**Image brief:** Wright-stained smear with erythrocytes containing single round basophilic inclusions, polychromasia, and scattered stain precipitate.

**Prompt:** Name the red-cell inclusion represented by a single, dense, round basophilic body near the cell periphery.

**Hardening target:** Differentiates Howell–Jolly bodies from precipitate and basophilic stippling.

## 15. Platelet estimate category
**Image brief:** Peripheral smear monolayer with countable platelet distribution, one platelet clump at the feathered edge, and giant platelets.

**Prompt:** Ignoring the platelet clump at the feathered edge, classify the platelet estimate in the monolayer as decreased, adequate, or increased.

**Hardening target:** Prevents clump-driven false adequacy and confusion of giant platelets with leukocyte debris.
