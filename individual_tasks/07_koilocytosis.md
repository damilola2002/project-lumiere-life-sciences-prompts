# 07 — Koilocytosis
**Image gate:** Pap smear with one true koilocyte and separate diffuse glycogen clearing.
**Prompt:** State the feature that makes the target cell’s perinuclear clearing diagnostic of koilocytosis.
**GTFA:** Sharply demarcated halo with a thickened irregular cytoplasmic rim
**Solution:** Step 1: Compare halo edges. Step 2: The target has a crisp rimmed halo. Step 3: That morphology is koilocytosis. **Final answer: Sharply demarcated halo with a thickened irregular cytoplasmic rim.**
**Distractors:** Diffuse clearing; pyknosis; orangeophilia; ciliary tuft; streaming.
**Failure target:** Calling any pale cytoplasm koilocytosis.
