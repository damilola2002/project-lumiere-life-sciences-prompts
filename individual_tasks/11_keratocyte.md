# 11 — Keratocyte
**Image gate:** Blood smear containing horned fragments, bite cells, echinocytes, and platelet clumps.
**Prompt:** Name the fragmented red-cell morphology with two sharp horn-like projections.
**GTFA:** Keratocyte
**Solution:** Step 1: Locate the horned fragmented cell. Step 2: Exclude evenly spiculated and singly notched cells. Step 3: The form is a keratocyte. **Final answer: Keratocyte.**
**Distractors:** Echinocyte; bite cell; spherocyte; target cell; elliptocyte.
**Failure target:** Conflating fragmentation with crenation.
