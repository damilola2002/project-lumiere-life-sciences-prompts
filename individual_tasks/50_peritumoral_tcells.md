# 50 — T-cell compartment
**Image gate:** Tissue map and high-power multiplex field across tumour-stroma boundary; T-cell-positive nuclei cluster outside tumour-marker nests.
**Prompt:** State the predominant spatial compartment of T-cell-marker-positive cells.
**GTFA:** Peritumoral stromal
**Solution:** Step 1: Locate tumour nests from marker/map. Step 2: Place T cells relative to nest boundaries. Step 3: They cluster in surrounding stroma. **Final answer: Peritumoral stromal.**
**Distractors:** Intratumoral; absent; intravascular only; necrotic core; epithelial lumen.
**Failure target:** Confusing peritumoral stroma with tumour boundary.
