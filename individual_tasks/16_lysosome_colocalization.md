# 16 — Lysosome colocalization
**Image gate:** Two-channel confocal cells: matching unsaturated red/green puncta and a separate saturated patch.
**Prompt:** State the cargo localization pattern in unsaturated cells.
**GTFA:** Punctate colocalization with lysosomes
**Solution:** Step 1: Exclude saturated pixels. Step 2: Match red and green puncta across channels. Step 3: Repeated overlap shows lysosomal colocalization. **Final answer: Punctate colocalization with lysosomes.**
**Distractors:** Diffuse separation; nuclear signal; saturation-only overlap; membrane signal; autofluorescence.
**Failure target:** Inferring colocalization from a merged saturated image.
