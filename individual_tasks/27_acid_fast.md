# 27 — Acid-fast bacilli
**Image gate:** Ziehl–Neelsen smear with slender beaded red rods and one broad red textile fibre.
**Prompt:** Which structure represents acid-fast bacilli?
**GTFA:** Slender beaded red rods
**Solution:** Step 1: Compare width and continuity. Step 2: Diagnostic forms are slender and beaded. Step 3: These are acid-fast bacilli. **Final answer: Slender beaded red rods.**
**Distractors:** Broad fibre; blue debris; air-bubble rim; epithelial edge; precipitate.
**Failure target:** Fibre contamination.
