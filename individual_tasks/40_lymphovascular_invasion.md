# 40 — Lymphovascular invasion
**Image gate:** H&E with tumour embolus in endothelial-lined channel and adjacent retraction cleft.
**Prompt:** State the finding that establishes lymphovascular invasion.
**GTFA:** Tumour nest in an endothelial-lined space
**Solution:** Step 1: Trace boundary around nest. Step 2: Identify endothelial lining. Step 3: An intraluminal nest establishes invasion. **Final answer: Tumour nest in an endothelial-lined space.**
**Distractors:** Retraction cleft; stromal invasion; perineural invasion; gland lumen; fold.
**Failure target:** Assuming every peritumour space is vascular.
