# 41 — Scaffold pore connectivity
**Image gate:** SEM shows pore openings linked by continuous channels, isolated pits, and charging streaks.
**Prompt:** What feature demonstrates an interconnected pore network?
**GTFA:** Openings linked by continuous channels
**Solution:** Step 1: Trace boundaries of adjacent pores. Step 2: Observe a continuous open channel. Step 3: This proves interconnectivity. **Final answer: Openings linked by continuous channels.**
**Distractors:** Dark pits; charging streaks; isolated pits; shadowed ridge; detector artifact.
**Failure target:** Confusing SEM artifact with topology.
