# 23 — Plasmid topology
**Image gate:** Gel sample with three plasmid forms and a linearized control at intermediate migration.
**Prompt:** Which plasmid topology migrates farthest relative to the linearized control?
**GTFA:** Supercoiled
**Solution:** Step 1: Compare plasmid bands with linear control. Step 2: The fastest band is most compact. Step 3: Supercoiled plasmid is most compact. **Final answer: Supercoiled.**
**Distractors:** Open circular; linear; nicked dimer; concatemer; primer dimer.
**Failure target:** Assuming larger apparent band always moves less.
