# 06 — Pap HSIL
**Image gate:** Pap smear with a crowded atypical cluster of high N:C cells plus mature superficial cells.
**Prompt:** Classify the atypical squamous cluster by its predominant morphology.
**GTFA:** High-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion
**Solution:** Step 1: Compare nuclear size with cytoplasm. Step 2: Note uniform high N:C ratio and irregular hyperchromasia. Step 3: These support HSIL. **Final answer: High-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion.**
**Distractors:** LSIL; ASC-US; atrophy; reactive metaplasia; carcinoma.
**Failure target:** Overweighting isolated hyperchromasia.
