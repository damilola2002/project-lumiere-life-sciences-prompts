# 26 — Gram morphology
**Image gate:** Thin well-decolorized smear with purple clustered cocci, pink rods, thick artifact, and precipitate.
**Prompt:** State the Gram reaction and arrangement of predominant cocci in the correctly decolorized field.
**GTFA:** Gram-positive cocci in clusters
**Solution:** Step 1: Use thin field. Step 2: Cells are purple, round, clustered. Step 3: This is Gram-positive clustered cocci. **Final answer: Gram-positive cocci in clusters.**
**Distractors:** Gram-negative diplococci; Gram-positive rods; Gram-negative rods; yeast; precipitate.
**Failure target:** Reading thick unde-colorized material.
