# 17 — Nuclear translocation
**Image gate:** DAPI, factor, and merge panels with factor overlapping DAPI in intact cells plus dead-cell bright objects.
**Prompt:** State the predominant localization of the transcription factor in viable cells.
**GTFA:** Nuclear
**Solution:** Step 1: Select intact DAPI-positive cells. Step 2: Compare factor signal to nuclear boundaries. Step 3: Signal is predominantly nuclear. **Final answer: Nuclear.**
**Distractors:** Cytoplasmic; membranous; mitochondrial; extracellular; dead-cell uptake.
**Failure target:** Incorporating dead cells into localization.
