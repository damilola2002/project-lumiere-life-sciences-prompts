# 20 — Dead-cell dye
**Image gate:** Viable calcein cells, membrane-impermeant dye-positive nuclei, and fluorescent debris.
**Prompt:** What biological state is identified by the membrane-impermeant nuclear dye?
**GTFA:** Cells with lost plasma-membrane integrity
**Solution:** Step 1: Locate dye-positive nuclei within cells. Step 2: The dye enters only compromised membranes. Step 3: It identifies membrane-integrity loss. **Final answer: Cells with lost plasma-membrane integrity.**
**Distractors:** Metabolically active cells; all nuclei; proliferating cells; lysosome-rich cells; debris.
**Failure target:** Counting debris as dead cells.
