# 45 — Matrix mineralization
**Image gate:** Alizarin Red field with adherent irregular nodules in monolayer and free angular dye crystals outside it.
**Prompt:** Which feature indicates matrix mineralization?
**GTFA:** Adherent irregular orange-red nodules associated with the cell layer
**Solution:** Step 1: Locate material relative to monolayer. Step 2: Target is attached irregular nodules, not free crystals. Step 3: This is mineralization. **Final answer: Adherent irregular orange-red nodules associated with the cell layer.**
**Distractors:** Free crystals; diffuse stain; bubble; cell-free precipitate; scratch.
**Failure target:** Dye precipitation.
