# 18 — Z-stack equator
**Image gate:** Three equal-scale z-planes of one spheroid; Plane 2 has the greatest continuous diameter.
**Prompt:** Which numbered z-plane passes closest to the spheroid equator?
**GTFA:** Plane 2
**Solution:** Step 1: Compare cross-sectional widths. Step 2: Plane 2 is largest. Step 3: Maximal profile is closest to equator. **Final answer: Plane 2.**
**Distractors:** Plane 1; Plane 3; all planes; surface plane; no plane.
**Failure target:** Treating brightness as depth rather than geometry.
