# 42 — Cell spreading
**Image gate:** Actin/nuclei image on coating plus a no-cell coating autofluorescence control.
**Prompt:** What feature demonstrates adherent cell spreading on the coating?
**GTFA:** Actin-positive flattened outlines surrounding nuclei
**Solution:** Step 1: Compare with material-only control. Step 2: Find flattened actin outlines around nuclei. Step 3: This demonstrates spreading. **Final answer: Actin-positive flattened outlines surrounding nuclei.**
**Distractors:** Diffuse coating fluorescence; bright specks; nuclear autofluorescence; halo; noise.
**Failure target:** Calling coating fluorescence cells.
