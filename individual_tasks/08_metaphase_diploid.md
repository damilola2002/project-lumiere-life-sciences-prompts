# 08 — Metaphase class
**Image gate:** Scorable G-banded spread with exactly 46 chromosomes, overlap, and debris.
**Prompt:** Classify the chromosome complement.
**GTFA:** Euploid diploid
**Solution:** Step 1: Count chromosome-shaped objects only. Step 2: Exclude debris and confirm 46. Step 3: Forty-six is euploid diploid. **Final answer: Euploid diploid.**
**Distractors:** Trisomy 21; monosomy X; near-tetraploid; triploidy; translocation.
**Failure target:** Counting debris or separate chromatids.
