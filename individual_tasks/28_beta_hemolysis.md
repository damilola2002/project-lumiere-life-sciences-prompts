# 28 — Beta hemolysis
**Image gate:** Blood-agar plate with a transparent clearing halo around target colony and green partial hemolysis elsewhere.
**Prompt:** Classify the hemolysis around the target colony.
**GTFA:** Beta hemolysis
**Solution:** Step 1: Inspect underlying agar. Step 2: Target zone is transparent, not green. Step 3: Complete clearing is beta hemolysis. **Final answer: Beta hemolysis.**
**Distractors:** Alpha hemolysis; gamma hemolysis; swarming; satellite growth; condensation.
**Failure target:** Glare or partial greening.
