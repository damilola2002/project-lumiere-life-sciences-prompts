# 03 — Caseating granuloma
**Image gate:** H&E lung granuloma with acellular granular eosinophilic centre, giant cells, and a nearby tissue fold.
**Prompt:** How should the granuloma be classified from its central morphology?
**GTFA:** Caseating granuloma
**Solution:** Step 1: Inspect the central zone. Step 2: It is granular, acellular, and necrotic. Step 3: Caseous necrosis defines this subtype. **Final answer: Caseating granuloma.**
**Distractors:** Noncaseating granuloma; suppurative granuloma; foreign-body granuloma; fibrinoid necrosis; tissue fold.
**Failure target:** Mistaking a pale fold for necrosis.
