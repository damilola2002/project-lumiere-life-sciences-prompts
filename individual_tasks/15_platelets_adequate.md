# 15 — Platelet estimate
**Image gate:** Ten monolayer fields averaging 8–15 platelets, a feather-edge clump, and giant platelets.
**Prompt:** Classify the platelet estimate after excluding the feather-edge clump.
**GTFA:** Adequate
**Solution:** Step 1: Use monolayer fields only. Step 2: Exclude the clump. Step 3: Representative density is adequate. **Final answer: Adequate.**
**Distractors:** Decreased; increased; pseudothrombocytopenic; platelet clumping; giant-platelet syndrome.
**Failure target:** Letting the edge clump determine the estimate.
