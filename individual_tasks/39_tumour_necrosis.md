# 39 — Tumour necrosis
**Image gate:** H&E tumour with viable rim, acellular eosinophilic geographic necrosis, hemorrhage, and clefts.
**Prompt:** Which region belongs in the tumour-necrosis estimate?
**GTFA:** Acellular eosinophilic geographic region
**Solution:** Step 1: Find loss of cellular detail. Step 2: Target is acellular/eosinophilic. Step 3: Include it as necrosis. **Final answer: Acellular eosinophilic geographic region.**
**Distractors:** Hemorrhage; processing clefts; viable rim; vessel lumen; fat.
**Failure target:** Confusing hemorrhage/clefts with necrosis.
