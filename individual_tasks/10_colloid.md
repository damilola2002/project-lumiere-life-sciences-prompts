# 10 — Colloid nodule background
**Image gate:** Thyroid FNA with flat follicular sheets and dense rope-like colloid.
**Prompt:** Name the background component supporting a benign colloid nodule.
**GTFA:** Thick ropy colloid
**Solution:** Step 1: Inspect extracellular material. Step 2: It is cohesive and rope-like, not granular. Step 3: This is colloid. **Final answer: Thick ropy colloid.**
**Distractors:** Tumour necrosis; tigroid background; mucin; amyloid; blood clot.
**Failure target:** Confusing colloid with smear artifact.
