# 04 — IHC compartment
**Image gate:** DAB/hematoxylin section with brown tumour nuclei and pigmented macrophage cytoplasm.
**Prompt:** State the subcellular compartment containing specific immunostain in tumour cells.
**GTFA:** Nuclear
**Solution:** Step 1: Identify tumour cells, not macrophages. Step 2: Compare DAB to nuclear boundaries. Step 3: Chromogen lies inside nuclei. **Final answer: Nuclear.**
**Distractors:** Cytoplasmic; membranous; extracellular; Golgi-pattern; pigment.
**Failure target:** Assigning cytoplasmic pigment to the tumour signal.
