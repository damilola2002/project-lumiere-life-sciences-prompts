# 25 — Spatial transcriptomics
**Image gate:** H&E plus spots: high expression overlays intact hypercellular tumour, not necrosis/stroma.
**Prompt:** Which histologic compartment corresponds to the high-expression spot cluster?
**GTFA:** Viable tumour
**Solution:** Step 1: Register spots to H&E. Step 2: High spots overlap intact tumour nests. Step 3: The compartment is viable tumour. **Final answer: Viable tumour.**
**Distractors:** Necrotic debris; stroma; adipose; vessel lumen; tissue fold.
**Failure target:** Mapping color intensity without tissue context.
