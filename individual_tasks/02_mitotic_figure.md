# 02 — Mitotic figure
**Image gate:** H&E tumour field with a true metaphase plate, apoptotic bodies, crushed nuclei, and precipitate.
**Prompt:** What is the morphologic category of the cell with chromosomes aligned in an equatorial plate?
**GTFA:** Mitotic figure
**Solution:** Step 1: Locate organized discrete chromosomes. Step 2: Exclude rounded fragmented apoptotic bodies. Step 3: Equatorial alignment identifies mitosis. **Final answer: Mitotic figure.**
**Distractors:** Apoptotic body; pyknosis; crush artifact; melanin; stain precipitate.
**Failure target:** Treating every hyperchromatic dot as mitosis.
