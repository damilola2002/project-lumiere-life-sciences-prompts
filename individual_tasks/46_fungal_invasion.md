# 46 — Fungal invasion
**Image gate:** Low-power H&E localizes necrosis; high-power special stain shows embedded branching hyphae in same focus.
**Prompt:** Classify the filamentous structures in the necrotic focus.
**GTFA:** Tissue-invasive fungal hyphae
**Solution:** Step 1: Register the focus across panels. Step 2: Trace filaments embedded in tissue. Step 3: Embedded hyphae establish invasion. **Final answer: Tissue-invasive fungal hyphae.**
**Distractors:** Surface artifact; collagen fibre; fold; mucus strand; precipitate.
**Failure target:** Cross-panel misregistration.
