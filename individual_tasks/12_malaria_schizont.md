# 12 — Malaria schizont
**Image gate:** Thin film with one erythrocyte containing multiple merozoites around pigment; platelets overlay nearby cells.
**Prompt:** Name the intraerythrocytic parasite stage shown by the multi-merozoite form.
**GTFA:** Schizont
**Solution:** Step 1: Restrict to bodies inside one erythrocyte. Step 2: Find multiple merozoites around pigment. Step 3: This defines a schizont. **Final answer: Schizont.**
**Distractors:** Ring trophozoite; gametocyte; platelet overlay; Howell–Jolly body; precipitate.
**Failure target:** Treating platelets as parasites.
