# 13 — Auer rod lineage
**Image gate:** Marrow smear with a blast containing a cytoplasmic needle-shaped Auer rod and staining streaks elsewhere.
**Prompt:** State the lineage supported by the true cytoplasmic Auer rod.
**GTFA:** Myeloid
**Solution:** Step 1: Confirm the needle lies within one blast. Step 2: Exclude streaks crossing cells. Step 3: Auer rods support myeloid lineage. **Final answer: Myeloid.**
**Distractors:** Lymphoid; plasma-cell; megakaryocytic; erythroid; monocytic only.
**Failure target:** Calling stain streaks Auer rods.
