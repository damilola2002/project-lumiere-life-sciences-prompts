# 09 — FISH amplification
**Image gate:** Intact nuclei with clustered red target signals and two green controls; include truncated nuclei.
**Prompt:** State the target-probe copy-number pattern in intact isolated nuclei.
**GTFA:** Target amplification
**Solution:** Step 1: Exclude truncated/overlapping nuclei. Step 2: Compare red signals with green controls. Step 3: Clustered red excess indicates amplification. **Final answer: Target amplification.**
**Distractors:** Target deletion; diploid pattern; polysomy; truncation; hybridization failure.
**Failure target:** Counting signals in truncated nuclei.
