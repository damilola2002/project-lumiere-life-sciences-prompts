# 14 — Howell–Jolly body
**Image gate:** Smear with a solitary dense round RBC inclusion and other cells with true basophilic stippling.
**Prompt:** Name the solitary round basophilic inclusion near the erythrocyte periphery.
**GTFA:** Howell–Jolly body
**Solution:** Step 1: Note it is single and round. Step 2: Contrast multiple stippled granules. Step 3: The solitary body is Howell–Jolly. **Final answer: Howell–Jolly body.**
**Distractors:** Basophilic stippling; Pappenheimer body; Heinz body; malaria ring; precipitate.
**Failure target:** Misclassifying a single inclusion as stippling.
