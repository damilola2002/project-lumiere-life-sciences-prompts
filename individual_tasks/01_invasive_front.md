# 01 — Invasive front
**Image gate:** H&E breast field: two irregular nests beyond a duct boundary in desmoplastic stroma; separate smooth retraction clefts.
**Prompt:** What histologic finding in the image establishes invasive carcinoma?
**GTFA:** Irregular tumour nests infiltrating desmoplastic stroma
**Solution:** Step 1: Find nests in reactive stroma. Step 2: Exclude cleft-rimmed in-situ nests. Step 3: Stromal infiltration establishes invasion. **Final answer: Irregular tumour nests infiltrating desmoplastic stroma.**
**Distractors:** Retraction artifact; comedo necrosis; DCIS; stromal edema; apocrine change.
**Failure target:** Confusing processing clefts with infiltration.
