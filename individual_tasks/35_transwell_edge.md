# 35 — Transwell edge artifact
**Image gate:** Crystal-violet membrane with scattered central cells and a continuous intensely stained perimeter ring.
**Prompt:** Which region must be excluded from migration quantification?
**GTFA:** Membrane-edge ring
**Solution:** Step 1: Compare centre/perimeter. Step 2: Perimeter is continuous noncellular stain. Step 3: Exclude the edge ring. **Final answer: Membrane-edge ring.**
**Distractors:** Central field; isolated cell; pore opening; cell cluster; granule.
**Failure target:** Counting rim stain as migrated cells.
