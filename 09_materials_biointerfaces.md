# Biomaterials and biointerfaces

## 41. SEM scaffold pore connectivity
**Image brief:** SEM micrograph of a porous scaffold with open pores, closed surface depressions, charging streaks, and a scale bar.

**Prompt:** Which visible feature provides evidence of an interconnected pore network rather than isolated surface pits: openings linked by continuous channels, uniformly dark depressions, or charging streaks?

**Hardening target:** Distinguishes topology from electron-imaging artifact.

## 42. Cell attachment on coating
**Image brief:** Fluorescence image of cells on a biomaterial coating with actin, nuclei, coating autofluorescence, and a no-cell material control.

**Prompt:** In the experimental panel, which feature demonstrates adherent cell spreading rather than coating autofluorescence: actin-positive flattened outlines surrounding nuclei, diffuse material fluorescence, or isolated bright specks?

**Hardening target:** Tests control-aware channel interpretation.

## 43. Contact-angle graph
**Image brief:** Contact-angle versus time plot for two surface treatments with distinct line styles, error bars, a broken y-axis, and a legend.

**Prompt:** Which treatment shows the larger decrease in contact angle over the recorded interval?

**Hardening target:** Requires correct legend binding and axis-break awareness.

## 44. Live/dead depth penetration
**Image brief:** Confocal cross-section through a cell-seeded hydrogel, with depth scale, live/dead channels, optical attenuation, and a bright surface layer.

**Prompt:** At which labelled depth is the first continuous viable-cell population visible beyond the superficial bright layer?

**Hardening target:** Distinguishes depth-resolved cells from surface saturation.

## 45. Mineralization stain
**Image brief:** Alizarin Red S brightfield image with orange-red calcium deposits, cell monolayer, dye crystals, and uneven illumination.

**Prompt:** Which feature should be interpreted as matrix mineralization: adherent irregular orange-red nodules associated with the cell layer or free angular dye crystals outside the monolayer?

**Hardening target:** Separates precipitated stain from biologic deposits.
