# Clinical and agricultural microbiology

## 26. Gram-stain interpretation
**Image brief:** Gram-stained smear with purple cocci in clusters, pink rods, stain precipitate, and a thick region with poor decolorization.

**Prompt:** In the well-spread, correctly decolorized portion of the image, what is the Gram reaction and arrangement of the predominant cocci?

**Hardening target:** Avoids interpreting the thick, under-decolorized region or precipitate.

## 27. Acid-fast bacilli
**Image brief:** Ziehl–Neelsen smear with slender red bacilli, blue background debris, red fibers, and uneven stain thickness.

**Prompt:** Which structure should be counted as acid-fast bacilli: slender, beaded red rods against the blue counterstain, or broad red fibers crossing the field?

**Hardening target:** Distinguishes bacilli from textile/fiber contamination.

## 28. Bacterial colony hemolysis
**Image brief:** Blood-agar plate photograph with distinct colonies, one zone of complete clearing, partial greening around another colony, condensation, and reflected glare.

**Prompt:** What hemolysis category is demonstrated by the colony surrounded by a transparent zone of complete red-cell clearing: alpha, beta, or gamma?

**Hardening target:** Separates true clearing from glare and green discoloration.

## 29. Fungal morphology in culture
**Image brief:** Lactophenol cotton blue mount with septate hyphae, conidiophores, rounded conidial heads, air bubbles, and broken hyphal fragments.

**Prompt:** Does the fungus in the image show septate hyphae or broad aseptate ribbon-like hyphae?

**Hardening target:** Requires visualising septa despite overlap and staining variation.

## 30. Food-spoilage stage
**Image brief:** Matched photographs of fruit at sequential storage times with surface mold, enzymatic browning, bruising, and condensation.

**Prompt:** Which numbered specimen has progressed to microbial spoilage rather than displaying only abiotic bruising, based on visible mycelial growth and soft collapse?

**Hardening target:** Distinguishes fungal growth from surface discoloration.
