## 6. Pap smear cell identity

## 9. FISH signal pattern
**Image brief:** Interphase FISH nuclei with red target and green control probes; include truncated nuclei, one overlapping nucleus pair, and autofluorescent specks.

**Prompt:** In the intact, non-overlapping nuclei shown, what target-to-control signal pattern is present: deletion, balanced diploid pattern, or target amplification?
